Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services.

What makes Queens Care Laboratories unique?

Queens Care Laboratories stands out due to our unwavering commitment to scientific rigor, patient-centric approach, and continuous innovation. We invest heavily in R&D to bring groundbreaking therapies to market, ensuring the highest standards of quality and safety.

Are your products FDA approved?

Yes, all our pharmaceutical products undergo stringent testing and comply with all relevant regulatory body guidelines, including FDA (or equivalent international bodies) approvals, before being released to the market. Patient safety and product efficacy are our top priorities.

How can I purchase your products?

Our products are primarily available through licensed pharmacies and healthcare providers. For specific product availability or to find a distributor near you, please contact our customer service team or consult with your healthcare professional.

Do you offer international shipping?

Queens Care Laboratories operates globally through a network of authorized distributors. Please check with your local distributor or contact us directly to inquire about product availability and shipping options in your region.
